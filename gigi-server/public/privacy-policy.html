Privacy Policy for Gigi App

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Gigi. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("Gigi" or the "App").

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of personal information to provide and improve our service:

  • Account Information: Phone number and email address for account authentication and communication.
  • Device Information: Device IDs and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens to deliver push notifications and mirror app states between connected devices.
  • User Content: Photos (including photos you take with the in-app camera), drawings (scribbles), love cards, and other content you explicitly share with your partner through the App.
  • Approximate Location (optional): If you grant location access, your location is shared with your connected partner to show presence and the distance between you. You can decline or revoke this permission at any time in your device settings; the App works without it.
  • Purchase Information: If you subscribe to a paid plan, the purchase is processed by Google Play. We receive and store only your subscription status (plan tier and expiry) — never your payment details.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To facilitate real-time connection and content sharing between you and your linked partner.
  • To send you notifications related to app activity.
  • To improve the functionality and performance of the App.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is securely stored. We employ industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information and shared content from unauthorized access. Only you and your connected partner have access to your shared private content.

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your information as long as your account is active. You can request to delete your account at any time within the App's Profile settings or by visiting our Account Deletion Page. Deleting your account will permanently erase your profile, active connections, and all shared content (photos, scribbles, cards) from our servers.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your shared content is only visible to the specific partner you connect with via the App.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@gigi.iamanraj.com.
