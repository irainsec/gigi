Delete Your Gigi Account

Fastest way: if you can still sign in, open the Gigi app → Profile → Delete Account. Deletion there is immediate.

Warning: Deleting your account is permanent. This will erase:

  • Your profile and login information
  • All your active connections with your partner(s)
  • Your shared scribbles, notes, and photos
  • Your love card decks

This data cannot be recovered once deleted.

If you can't access the app, request deletion here. Enter the phone number or Google email registered with your account; your account and all associated data are permanently deleted within 7 days.

Questions? Contact support@gigi.iamanraj.com.
